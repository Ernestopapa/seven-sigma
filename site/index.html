What a Sigma Node is

A licence, not just art

A Sigma Node is a verifiable compute license. Each of the 777 NFTs is one node in a Cognitive Orderflow Mesh. Seven autonomous sub-agents live inside each Node, one for each edge: basis trade, funding-rate harvest, volatility convexity, cross-market arbitrage, TWAP stealth execution, MEV-shielded routing, and tail-risk hedging. Honest notes: the contract is NOT audited by a third party. It uses OpenZeppelin ERC721 + ERC721Royalty + Ownable + ReentrancyGuard. The owner can withdraw BNB and change metadata via setBaseURI, and can transfer or renounce ownership. The owner cannot change supply (777), free supply (150), price (0.00042 BNB), max 3 per transaction, or the Agent ID registry, and cannot mint extra NFTs, pause, freeze, burn, or take holders' NFTs. Royalties are 5% ERC-2981 to the owner, but some marketplaces ignore ERC-2981. No floor price, returns, or APY is promised.
